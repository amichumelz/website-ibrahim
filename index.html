Booking terms

Booking

Book at least 1 day before your activity. Send us your name, phone number, number of guests, activity date and time.

Payment

Full payment (100%) is required upon booking for all car transport and tour packages to confirm your vehicle, assigned driver, and activity tickets.

Tickets

E-tickets or vouchers are issued after payment is confirmed. Issued tickets are non-transferable and non-refundable.

Cancellation

More than 48 hours before: partial refund, subject to the operator. Within 48 hours or no-show: non-refundable. If the operator cancels for weather or safety, you can reschedule or request a refund.

Prices include

Entry or participation fee and the standard inclusions listed in each package (safety gear, boat ride, guide).

Prices exclude

Dayang Bunting Lake entrance (RM15–RM20 per person), Bat Cave entrance (RM2 per person), meals and drinks unless stated, hotel pickup unless stated, insurance, tips and personal expenses.

Prices are valid until 12 December 2026 and may change with government regulations, operator updates or fuel price adjustments.
